The Framework

Himat-Hikmat.
Courage and Wisdom.

The intellectual architecture of the Enlyst Foundation. Not a slogan — a research methodology. Two pillars that must hold simultaneously for institutional reform to be honest.

H
Pillar One

Himat — Courage

The courage to see systems as they are — not as they are described in policy documents. The courage to name the failure, trace its incentive structure, and resist the institutional pressure to soften the diagnosis.

Structural diagnosis before prescription — always
Full institutional audit before any intervention design
Name the incentives that produce the failure, not just the failure
Resistance to displacing data with narrative
H
Pillar Two

Hikmat — Wisdom

The wisdom to design alternatives that survive contact with reality. Evidence-anchored, testable, proportionate. Knowing the limits of what data can claim. Building what is implementable, not just what is ideal.

Evidence-anchored alternatives — not aspirational ones
Human cost as the primary metric — not administrative convenience
Testable, falsifiable policy recommendations
Proportionate interventions matched to system capacity

Foundation Principle

Structure

Ring-fenced.
By design.

The Foundation is not a CSR line item. It is constitutively separate from the commercial P&L — board-protected, metric-distinct, and donor-auditable. Commercial cycles cannot silently consume the mission.

01

Board Protection

Foundation decisions are insulated from commercial P&L pressure. The board holds the mission, not the revenue cycle.

02

Metric Distinctness

Foundation outcomes are measured in research rigour, policy reach, and human impact — not in commercial ROI.

03

Donor Auditability

Every research output, field deployment, and policy recommendation is documented and available for donor review.

04

Commercial Cross-Subsidy

Retainer revenue from the commercial stack funds the Foundation's research rigour. The Foundation does not depend on external grants for its independence.
