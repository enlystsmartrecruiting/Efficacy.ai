02 · The Framework

Himat Without Hikmat
is Courage Without Direction

The Himat-Hikmat Framework identifies a structural investment asymmetry at the heart of Punjab's special education policy: the state has mastered the welfare dimension while systematically neglecting the pedagogical dimension.

⚡
Himat
Courage · Financial Support · Welfare

The state's financial investment in students with visual impairments. Substantial, institutionalised, and growing. The "hardware" of inclusion.

PKR 56+ billion
Annual welfare expenditure
  • Himat Card — PKR 10,500/quarter per student
  • 85,000+ registered beneficiaries across Punjab
  • New school buildings and transport
  • Uniform and assistive device distribution
  • Examination fee waivers and accommodations
  • 2% reserved job quota in government departments
VS
🎓
Hikmat
Wisdom · Pedagogical Knowledge · Teaching Quality

The state's investment in specialist teaching capability. Negligible, uncoordinated, and declining. The "software" of inclusion — absent.

PKR 168 million
Annual teacher training expenditure
  • No specialist pre-service VI mathematics curriculum
  • 0 days of Nemeth Braille training in standard BEd
  • No Braille mathematics resource centres
  • No peripheral posting incentives
  • 64.4% of teachers: below-proficiency PCK scores
  • M = 2.14/4.0 on teacher self-efficacy scale
⚠

The Himat Disconnect

Financial support enables a student to arrive at school. It cannot teach them mathematics. A Himat Card stipend that enables a student with visual impairment to attend school where the teacher cannot read Braille merely funds presence — not learning. The state subsidises attendance but withholds the instructional competence that makes attendance worthwhile. This is the structural contradiction this research documents, measures, and calls upon policy to resolve.

03 · Original Theory

The PCK-Efficacy Nexus

Specialist pedagogical knowledge and domain-specific teacher self-efficacy are bidirectionally linked. Without one, the other cannot develop. When both are absent from initial preparation, a self-reinforcing deficit cycle forms — and persists.

04 · Geographic Disparities

Same Province. Same Exam.
34.3 Points Apart.

Division-Level Matriculation Pass Rates

Central Divisions Excel

Lahore, Rawalpindi, and Faisalabad — with access to professional development, university resources, and higher teacher retention — achieve pass rates between 85–100%.

Peripheral Divisions Struggle

D.G. Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal lag severely — not from curriculum differences or welfare disparities, but from teacher efficacy differentials. The correlation between division-level efficacy and achievement is r = .71.

Welfare Cannot Bridge the Gap

Himat Card stipends are uniform across all divisions. The 34.3-point gap persists because welfare addresses attendance, not instructional quality. This is the framework's empirical proof point.

Teacher Variables Explain Variance

PCKA-VI scores differ significantly across divisions (F(8,81) = 7.23, p < .001). Lahore teachers average 9.2/12; D.G. Khan teachers average 4.8/12. Geography reflects teacher preparation, not student capacity.

06 · Qualitative Findings

Four Themes from the Field

THEME 01
🔒
Resource Scarcity as Instructional Paralysis
Teachers cannot adapt what they do not have. Absent Braille textbooks, tactile manipulatives, and accessible graphing tools, even motivated and skilled teachers are constrained to verbal instruction alone — effectively excluding visual content from mathematics teaching.
"I explain everything with words. There is no other way. My students have never touched a geometry diagram."
THEME 02
📖
Nemeth Code Illiteracy as the Foundational Deficit
64.4% of teachers cannot write or read Braille mathematics. The Nemeth Code is not a supplementary skill — it is the medium of mathematical communication for blind learners. Its absence from teacher preparation is not an oversight; it is a systemic failure of curriculum design.
"I passed my BEd with distinction. Nobody taught me a single Braille symbol. Not one."
THEME 03
🌍
Professional Isolation in Peripheral Divisions
Bandura's vicarious learning requires models to observe. In D.G. Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal, there are no expert colleagues, no professional development events, no visiting specialists. Isolation does not merely limit growth — it actively erodes existing confidence as teachers receive no validation of their practice.
"I have worked here eight years. I have never met another special education mathematics teacher."
THEME 04
⚖️
Divergent Efficacy Responses Under Constraint
Facing identical structural constraints, teachers diverge into two camps. Adaptive resourcefulness: improvise with available materials, build peer networks, develop personal Braille competence independently. Learned helplessness: withdraw from challenge, minimise instructional ambition, attribute student failure to impairment rather than instruction.
"I made my own tactile diagrams from cardboard. If I waited for the department, my students would have nothing."

🔴
The Welfare Trap Argument
Every PKR 18,000 stipend paid to a student who doesn't develop genuine mathematical competence becomes a down-payment on a lifetime of welfare dependency. The state is not investing in education — it is financing the appearance of inclusion.
🟢
The Human Capital Argument
A visually impaired STEM graduate earning PKR 700,000 per year pays taxes, reduces welfare load, and contributes to the formal economy for 30+ years. The PCK investment that made this possible costs less per beneficiary than one year of university tuition.
🟡
The Policy Measurement Problem
Government currently measures success at the school door (enrollment, attendance). The fiscal losses occur silently — at the labor market door, 5–10 years later. By that point, no one traces the loss back to the teacher who never learned Nemeth Code.
The state has provided himat.
The evidence now demands hikmat.

Punjab's government faces a binary choice. Both paths spend the same stipend money. Only one converts that spending into human capital. The other converts it into permanent fiscal liability.
