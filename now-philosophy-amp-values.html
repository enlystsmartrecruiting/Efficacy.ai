Values

Hard constraints, not preferences.

These are criteria against which decisions are evaluated — not wall art.

01 · Belonging

Every person belongs in this conversation.

We are not gatekeepers. We protect the process that makes talent visible.

02 · Clarity

Vague feedback is a form of dishonesty.

Shortlists and declines are specific. If we cannot fill a role, we say so.

03 · Permanence

Build for longevity.

Systems should outlast the engagement. No quick wins that fail at scale.

04 · Systems protect processes

Technology frees judgment.

Reduce bias in the system so humans can protect humans — not drown in admin.

05 · Audit separation

Advisory and audit cannot coexist cleanly.

Audit-level work stays with independent firms. Architectural constraint, not a poster.

In the field

Operators, not a vanity roster.

Public bios stay lean. Engagements are led by practitioners who answer for process quality — starting with the founder.
