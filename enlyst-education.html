Why This Exists

Education that costs
nothing to access.
Everything to ignore.

"The Enlyst LMS is not a corporate training portal. It is a platform specifically engineered to empower underprivileged individuals who simply cannot afford the steep costs of modern technological education."

This extends the same founding principle that drives every Enlyst practice: a less biased society produces more owners than employees. Financial barriers to learning are a form of institutional bias. The LMS is our answer to that bias.

We partnered with global tech titans and structured the cost model so that the education is largely free — with a nominal domain account fee applied only on a strict case-by-case basis. Learning should not be a luxury.

Radical Cost Structure

Free to learn.
Earned to certify.

Enlyst has practically eliminated the financial barrier to entry. The education is largely free. The organisation charges a nominal domain account fee only on a strict case-by-case basis — and never as a precondition for learning.

This is not charity. It is a deliberate business architecture: a learner who completes a pathway becomes a potential candidate for the Graduate Internship Program, and eventually a future entrepreneur or employer who returns to the ecosystem.

Cost Model
Learning: Free
Account fee: Case-by-case

Certifications, pathways, and course content are free by default. The nominal account fee — only where applicable — covers domain provisioning and is never a barrier to access.

Graduate Internship Program

From learning
to doing business
by doing business.

The Graduate Internship Program is a 6 to 12-month paid apprenticeship where fresh graduates learn by actively working on real startup accounts — not simulated exercises. It is not charity. It is a tactical labour pipeline with three strategic exit paths.

Interns rotate across all four Enlyst subsidiaries: HR, Finance, Marketing, and Technology. This creates cross-functional professionals who understand how a business actually operates — a rare commodity in the modern workforce.

Three Exit Paths
Path A · Internal Absorption
Become an Enlyst Consultant

Top performers are fast-tracked into full-time Consultant or Manager roles within Enlyst — eliminating recruitment costs for the firm and rewarding demonstrated performance.

Path B · The Warm Handoff
Hired by the Client

Because interns work on specific client accounts, they are often permanently hired by the client — already trained, already culturally aligned. This increases client stickiness dramatically.

Path C · Graduate Entrepreneur
Launch Your Own Startup

Interns with founder DNA receive seed mentorship to launch their own startups. Those startups become new Enlyst clients — creating a self-perpetuating revenue cycle and a new generation of Pakistani entrepreneurs.
