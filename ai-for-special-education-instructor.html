Your Prompt Formula

CRTF — Use It Every Time

CContext

Who is the student — grade, disability, situation

RRole

Who should the AI act as — a teacher, a therapist, an editor

TTask

The one clear outcome you want

FFormat

How you want it structured — list, table, paragraph

The One Rule That Never Bends

Never type a real student's name, story or personal detail into any AI tool. Use "a student" or "the class" instead. You validate every output — AI never decides.

Digital Accessibility

One Adaptation Per Need

  • Visual ImpairmentNo visual-only instructions — screen readers and Be My Eyes AI
  • Hearing ImpairmentNo audio-only content — live captioning, text-first materials
  • Intellectual DisabilitySimplify and repeat — AI-generated simplified reading levels
  • ADHDChunk content, build in movement breaks
  • AutismStructure and predictability — visual schedules, social stories

Beyond These Two Days

This is a starting point, not the finish line. Pick one tool from the list above and use it once this week on something real you were already going to do — a worksheet, a letter, a lesson plan. That single repetition will teach you more than any slide did. When you're ready to go further, explore on your own terms — there is no single "right" course to take next.
