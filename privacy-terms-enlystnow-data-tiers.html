How our data model works

Two tiers, one principle: least data held.

Our public marketing sites are deliberately data-light. Operational systems that must hold personal data run under stricter controls, separated from anything public.

Tier 1 — Marketing sites

enlystnow.com, enlybiz.com, enlysoft.net and enlysum.com collect only what you submit through an enquiry or contact form (name, email, message). No tracking profiles are built and no personal data is sold.

Tier 2 — Operational systems

Products such as EnlyRecruit run on secured Google Cloud infrastructure with role-based access, encrypted transit, audit logging and EOBI-aligned handling. Access is limited to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis.

What we collect

Form submissions (contact details and your message), plus standard server logs (IP, timestamp, user agent) retained only for security and abuse prevention. Marketing pages set no advertising cookies.

How we use it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, deliver a requested service, and meet legal or regulatory obligations. We do not use enquiry data for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. Email info@enlystnow.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Sharing & processors

We share data only with infrastructure providers required to run our services (e.g. cloud hosting and email delivery) and where legally compelled. These processors are bound to protect your data.
