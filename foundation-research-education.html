Teaching Teachers of Disabled Students

Taking the Himat-Hikmat research into classrooms through structured capacity-building workshops across Punjab.

Workshop Design

Built from the evidence base. Sessions are structured around specific competency gaps — targeted capacity building tied to measurable outcomes.

Who Attends

Teachers from government and private special education schools. Priority cohorts include those with high VI enrolment and low pass rates.

What We Measure

Pre- and post-workshop competency assessments. Every workshop produces a dataset that feeds back into improving both research and training.
