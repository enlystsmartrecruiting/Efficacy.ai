About

Enlystnow is the mother platform.
The arms are siblings.

We are done reinventing who the parent is. Enlystnow sits above four equal practices — enlyst (HR), enlysum (Finance), enlybiz (Marketing), enlysoft (Tech). Institutional Enlyst Holding is a separate framing; it is not this site.

Philosophy

We don't sell software.
We install systems that protect your processes.

Every person carries the seed of an owner. We build infrastructure that makes pipelines auditable and frees specialists to protect — not merely process.

Think

Strategy first

Workforce planning and behavioural science before you post a single role.

Act

Accountable automation

Agentic sourcing, Kanban ATS, AI-assisted scorecards — with humans on the hard calls.

Protect

Compliance as design

EOBI, Social Security, contracts, verified pipelines — guardrails at scale.

What we stand on

Five values.
Non-negotiable.

01

Belonging

Every candidate and client belongs in the conversation. We are not gatekeepers.

02

Clarity

Vague feedback is a form of dishonesty. Assessments are specific and justified.

03

Permanence

We build for longevity — not transaction velocity.

04

Honesty first

We tell clients when we cannot fill a role. Candour over comfort.

05

Service as legacy

Every engagement should leave the client stronger than before we arrived.

Naming
