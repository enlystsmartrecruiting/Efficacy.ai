The Investor Readiness Problem

Investors do not fund
ideas. They fund evidence.

The most common reason a VC process stalls is not valuation disagreement. It is documentation that cannot survive due diligence — financial statements that are inconsistent, projections that are not anchored to historical performance, and a cap table that has not been properly maintained since formation.

enlysum's VC Readiness service prepares the financial documentation before the investor conversation begins. The objective is simple: when a professional investor's finance team opens your data room, every question they could ask has been anticipated and answered in the documentation itself.

This is not financial window-dressing. It is the discipline of organising what your business has already built into the format that investors are trained to read. The numbers do not change. The story becomes legible.

Financial Statement Clean-Up

Historical financial statements reviewed and restated where necessary for consistency. Accounting policy documentation. Auditor-readiness assessment. Balance sheet reclassifications that are standard for investor review.

Three-Year Financial Projection

Revenue model with documented assumptions. Cost model tied to headcount plan and operational drivers. Three scenarios — conservative, base, optimistic. Every assumption visible and challengeable.

Cap Table Documentation

Shareholder register verified. ESOP pool documented. Convertible notes and SAFEs tracked. Pre-money and post-money cap table produced for the raise. Waterfall analysis for the exit scenarios the investor will run.

Data Room Assembly

All documents organised into the standard VC data room structure: financial, legal, corporate, commercial, team. Index produced. Access permissions configured. Ready to share with a link in 48 hours.

What Belongs in a Data Room

Financial
3-year historical P&L · Balance sheet · Cash flow · Management accounts · Tax returns · Audited financials (if available)
Projections
3-year model with documented assumptions · Monthly cash flow · Break-even analysis · Use of funds post-raise
Corporate
SECP incorporation certificate · Shareholder register · Cap table · Board minutes · Employment agreements
Commercial
Key customer contracts · MRR/ARR analysis · Churn data · Pipeline · Unit economics per customer segment
