Resource Person Notes — Two-Day Strategy

  • Open by naming what's already AI in their daily life — mixed-seniority rooms need a shared floor, not a shared ceiling
  • Fold ethics into every demo rather than isolating it as one lecture — it lands as practice, not theory
  • State the "no real student data" rule once, clearly, early on Day 2 — don't bury it in the ethics slot
  • Keep every tool on free tiers only — assume no budget across the department
  • End Day 1 on the live demonstration — it is what makes people show up for Day 2
  • Save Digital Accessibility for the very last topic — it's your differentiator, not a footnote
  • Have a pre-recorded screen capture ready as backup for any live demo
  • Close with a verbal one-thing commitment from each participant, not just a takeaway handout

Digital Accessibility — Live Reference

One Adaptation, Every Disability Type

Visual Impairment

No visual-only instructions. Demonstrate Be My Eyes AI and screen-reader-friendly document structure.

Hearing Impairment

No audio-only content. Demonstrate Otter.ai live captioning and text-first material.

Intellectual Disability

Simplify and repeat. Demonstrate AI-generated simplified-reading-level worksheets.

ADHD

Chunk content, build in movement. Demonstrate AI breaking one long task into short steps.

Autism

Structure and predictability. Demonstrate AI-generated visual schedules and social stories.
