How It Works

From application
to placed.

Every Education programme follows the same four-step cohort model. Competitive entry. Structured delivery. Employer linkage before exit. Verified record after.

01

Apply

Structured application with aptitude screening. Each cohort has defined capacity — 12 for Talent Forge, 20 for Academy. Applications reviewed personally.

02

Learn

Structured curriculum delivered over 4–8 weeks. Assessment at each module gate. No passive learning — all exercises are real work product.

03

Match

Employer partners brief the cohort at week 4. Placement match confirmed before final week. No cold job searches on exit.

04

Certify

Enlyst Framework certification issued via Credly. Recognised across all four practices as a verified hire-ready record — not a participation certificate.
