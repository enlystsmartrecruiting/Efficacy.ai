The enterprise behind the mother

Built from
recruitment up.

Enlystnow is the public mother umbrella of an integrated multi-service enterprise that grew from recruitment into a mission-critical startup enabler. The arc was deliberate — talent is the first problem every founder faces, which gave us the trust to enter their operations, and the obligation to solve what we found there.

Under the leadership of Wasim Hassan, the enterprise partners with founders to construct sustainable businesses, transition them into permanent clients, and simultaneously cultivate the next generation of entrepreneurs. We don't just place people — we build businesses.

The Enlyst Framework is how we keep four operating arms — HR, finance, marketing, technology — coherent without erasing their distinct mandates. One accountability standard. One shared client memory. Four specialist teams that never need to re-brief each other.

What that means in practice
For Founders
Operating partner

Four arms under one Framework — HR, finance, marketing, technology — so you stop stitching vendors together and start operating as a single, reinforced institution. One retainer. One shared memory across everything you're building.

For Graduates
Forge, not hire

Paid apprenticeships across real client work. Talent is forged through Rotational Pedagogy — not screened from a CV pile alone. You leave with documented competency, not an attendance certificate.

For the Nation
Entrepreneurial scaffolding

Interns become leaders, client hires, or founders we incubate. The loop perpetuates itself. Each cohort feeds the next. The ecosystem compounds — and so does Pakistan's institutional capacity.

Mission
Partner with founders.
Build the
backbone.

Partner with founders to construct sustainable businesses. Move them from chaos into a Safe Zone Build, then into permanent retainer. At the same time, run a graduate forge that feeds the ecosystem with culturally aligned operators.

This is not a positioning statement. It is an operational description of what happens, in sequence, across every client engagement — from first conversation to year five of a retainer.

The outcome we design for

From a commission-based shop with fragile retention into a mission-critical partner with resilient, recurring revenue and 90%+ retention where the architecture holds. Not because we lock clients in — because removing Enlyst would mean removing HR, finance, marketing, and technology simultaneously.
