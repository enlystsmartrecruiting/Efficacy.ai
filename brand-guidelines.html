03

Usage rules

Minimum size: 88px wide for wordmark lockups, 32px for the mark alone. Clear space: minimum one mark-height on all sides.

Do

  • Use primary-light on off-white / cream / white surfaces
  • Use primary-dark on Obsidian (#1A1A1A) or any near-black surface
  • Use mono-white only on a division's own brand color or a photo/gradient background
  • Keep enlysoft on Tech Dark (#080C12) as its default surface — it is dark-first, not an inverted variant
  • Maintain the 100/58/26% opacity ramp on the mark exactly as specified

Don't

  • Recolor the mark outside its assigned division hue
  • Mix division colors within a single lockup
  • Use mono-white on light or off-white backgrounds — contrast fails
  • Stretch, skew, or alter the trapezoid angles
  • Apply drop shadows or outlines to the mark itself
  • Substitute the EF badge as a division logo
