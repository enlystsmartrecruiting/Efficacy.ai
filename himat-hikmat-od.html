The Himat-Hikmat Matrix

Chapter 2: Theoretical Framework

Why is the pipeline leaking? The study proposes that Himat (Hardware/Resources) creates access, but Hikmat (Software/Competence) creates learning. We are currently trapped in the top-left quadrant.

1. Zone of Neglect

Low Resources + Low Competence.
Total System Failure.

2. The Welfare Trap

High Resources + Low Competence.
Current State: Dependency & Waste.

4. Empowerment Zone

High Resources + High Competence.
The Goal: Mastery & Independence.
