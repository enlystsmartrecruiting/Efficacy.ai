Free Solar Installation Cost Estimate (2023 Installation Dates)

Ready to go solar in Alberta, BC, Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, PEI, or Saskatchewan? Then fill out the form below for a free, personalized solar installation cost estimate.*

*Note that your information will be forwarded to our hand-picked and vetted installation partner.

What 3 things do we use your information for?

1) Site Assessment & Modeling

The first thing that we do is perform an initial site assessment by looking at your shading, roof pitch, and roof direction. If you’re qualified, we will create a 2D or 3D model of your home (depending on image availability) with an appropriate amount of solar panels to match your power bill history. If you live on a farm or acreage we can model a ground-mount system.

2) Energy Output Calculation

Now that we have a model created, we will run a 1 year simulation using irradiance (sunlight) data for the exact geographic location of your home. We take into account not just the weather and sun location, but also everything included in the 3D model (shading, roof pitch, roof direction).

3) Financial Reporting

Once we know how much energy your system will produce, we can generate extremely accurate financial reports. For the best results we build in data from your energy provider including distribution, transmission, and rate fees as well as historical rate increases. We’ll calculate your 25-year savings, payback period, internal rate of return, and net present value (let us know your preferred discount rate) as needed. It’s that simple, why not get started today!
