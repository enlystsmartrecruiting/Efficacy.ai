Startup Framework

If you are a startup and you are ready to be serious — so are we.

Enlyst is not a startup accelerator. It is not a co-working space, a pitch competition, or a mentorship programme. It is an operational partner — and for the right startup, that is worth more than all of those combined.

The Safe Zone for Startups

How it works when a company contacts us.

Every startup engagement follows the same three-phase Safe Zone methodology — the same process we use for enterprises and government departments, calibrated for startup scale and speed.

01

Discovery & Audit

Duration: 2–3 weeks.

We map everything you have — product state, team structure, cash position, tech debt, brand positioning, compliance gaps, and operational bottlenecks. No assumptions. We start from evidence.

  • HR and team gap analysis
  • Finance and compliance audit
  • Brand and digital presence review
  • Technology infrastructure assessment
02

The Safe Zone Build

Duration: 3–4 months.

Parallel execution across your critical functions. We do not sequence — we build everything at once using the holding company model. Your startup gets the operational backbone it needs without waiting 12 months to hire for each role.

  • Hire your first 5–10 people via Enlyst HR
  • Set up finance systems via Enlysum
  • Launch your growth engine via Enlybiz
  • Deploy cloud infrastructure via Enlysoft
03

Permanent Partnership

Duration: Ongoing retainer.

Transition to a sustained monthly retainer. Continuous KPI reviews, quarterly strategy alignment, AI infrastructure maintenance, and ongoing talent support. We are not a project vendor — we become embedded in your operations.

  • Monthly KPI and financial review
  • Ongoing talent pipeline access
  • AI infrastructure monitoring
  • Strategic advisory and policy navigation

What kind of startup is right for Enlyst.

We are selective. We work with very few startups at any one time. This is intentional — we embed deeply, and that takes capacity.

A good fit for Enlyst

  • You have a product or service with early traction — not a pre-revenue idea
  • You want operational infrastructure built, not advice delivered
  • You are in a sector where Enlyst has domain experience — tech, services, education, healthcare, or public-sector adjacent
  • You are willing to commit to a structured engagement, not a short project
  • You want your team built correctly the first time — using behavioural science, not CVs
  • You are open to cloud-native infrastructure and AI integration from the outset

Not a fit right now

  • You are at idea stage with no validation — speak to an accelerator first
  • You want a one-time deliverable — a logo, a website, a pitch deck
  • You expect Enlyst to work on success-only or equity-only terms
  • You want generic digital marketing without an operational backbone
  • You are not willing to share financial and operational data — the audit requires transparency

What happens when you contact us.

A clear, no-pressure sequence — from first message to engagement decision.

1

Initial Message

Email or call with a brief description of your company, where you are, and what you need. No deck required at this stage.

2

Scoping Call (30 min)

We speak with the founder directly. We ask about your product, your team, your financials, and your operational pain points. We are honest about whether we are the right fit.

3

Discovery Proposal

If the fit is right, we send a scoped Discovery & Audit proposal — fixed cost, defined timeline, and a clear deliverable: a full operational assessment of your company.

4

Engagement Decision

After the audit, both parties decide whether to proceed to the Safe Zone Build. If we do, we move immediately. No pitch rounds. No waiting.
