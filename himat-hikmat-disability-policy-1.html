The Software: Instructional Failure

Despite the financial influx, the classroom experience remains impoverished. Teachers lack the specific tools and training ("Hikmat") required to teach students with visual impairments, leading to a "training illusion" where credentials exist without capability.

The Result: The Leaky Pipeline

Because teachers cannot teach the content (Software failure), students who are paid to attend (Hardware success) eventually fail.

24.3% of students drop out or fail between Grade 8 and Grade 10. This creates a dependency trap where students become lifelong welfare beneficiaries instead of independent contributors.

Dependency Trap

Key Recommendations

  • 1. Instructional Materials: Spend PKR 1.8 million (0.021% of budget) to equip all 36 schools.
  • 2. Teacher Training: Shift from generic workshops to specialized Nemeth Code & Pedagogy training.
  • 3. Balanced Metrics: Measure success by learning outcomes, not just enrollment numbers.
