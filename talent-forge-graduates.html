The Programme

Six months that
change the trajectory.

Pakistan produces more graduates than the market can absorb without structure. Talent Forge provides that structure — screening, matching, training, placing, and following up. We own the outcome, not just the process.

Month 01
01

Screen & Match

Competency-based entry screening. Track matching based on aptitude, not just degree.

Month 02
02

Foundation Skills

Professional writing, tools literacy, workplace ethics, and communication frameworks.

Month 03
03

Track Training

Deep-dive into your chosen track — HR, Tech, Finance, or Marketing. Real tools, real projects.

Month 04
04

Live Projects

Work on real client assignments under supervision. Build a documented portfolio, not just a CV.

Month 05
05

Employer Matching

Structured introductions to hiring companies. Behavioural screening applied both ways.

Month 06
06

Placement & Follow-up

Offer management and 90-day post-placement accountability. The programme ends when you're stable.

Four Tracks

professional direction.

Sourcing, screening methodology, behavioural interviewing, HRIS tools, EOBI compliance, and pipeline management. You will be placed as a junior recruiter or HR coordinator.
