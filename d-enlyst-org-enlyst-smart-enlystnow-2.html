Google Cloud Pathway

The Google Cloud Skills for Partners Track

Enlyst is a Google Cloud APAC Partner (DRP ID: ENLWHUGZ3) with 467,000+ Google Skills for Partners points and 147 Credly badges. This credential stack was built through live project work — and we use the same methodology to develop graduate interns.

Graduates on the Google track are assigned to live Enlysoft cloud projects — government infrastructure, GenAI deployments, and MCP server operations. Certification preparation is woven into the delivery work, not done in isolation.

GCP 1

Google Cloud Foundations

Cloud computing concepts, GCP console, IAM, storage, compute — completed alongside real project onboarding.

GCP 2

Associate Cloud Engineer

Deploy and manage GCP infrastructure. Prepare through Qwiklabs and active work on Enlysoft government deployments.

GCP 3

Professional Cloud Architect or Data Engineer

Advanced specialisation aligned to your project stream — GovTech infrastructure, data pipelines, or GenAI systems.

GCP 4

Google Skills for Partners Points Programme

Accumulate partner points through Cloudskillsboost. Tracked against Enlyst's partner KPIs — interns contribute directly to the firm's partner tier advancement.

M

Microsoft Pathway

The Microsoft Azure & M365 Track

Enlysoft's government infrastructure runs on both GCP and Azure. Graduates on the Microsoft track are embedded in Azure-hosted government microservices, M365 productivity deployments, and Copilot integration projects — earning Microsoft Credentials while doing real delivery work.

Credentials are earned through Microsoft Learn, supplemented by Enlyst-specific project assignments that map directly to certification domains. All study is done on funded accounts — graduates pay nothing for platform access.

AZ 1

AZ-900: Azure Fundamentals

Cloud concepts, Azure services, pricing, and SLA — completed in Week 1 of the programme before any project assignment.

AZ 2

AZ-104: Azure Administrator

Manage Azure subscriptions, identities, storage, and virtual machines — mapped directly to Enlysoft's government infrastructure deployments.

AZ 3

MS-900: Microsoft 365 Fundamentals

M365 productivity, Teams, SharePoint, and Copilot integration — applied to government department productivity deployments.

AZ 4

AI-900 / AI-102: Azure AI Fundamentals & Engineer

Azure AI services, OpenAI integration, and responsible AI design — directly applicable to Enlysoft's MCP server and GovTech AI projects.

How the programme works.

A structured 6-month internship with a clear certification milestone, live project delivery, and a defined placement outcome.

01

Weeks 1–2: Onboarding

Framework orientation, team placement, and AZ-900 or GCP Foundations certification. Access to Enlyst client systems and project briefings.

02

Months 1–3: Live Delivery

Embedded on a real Enlysoft or government project — contributing to active infrastructure, data pipelines, or AI deployments. Certification track runs in parallel with weekly study sessions.

03

Months 4–5: Specialisation

Advance to your track's primary certification (Associate Cloud Engineer, AZ-104, or equivalent). Take on a defined sub-project with direct client visibility and a deliverable outcome.

04

Month 6: Placement

Portfolio review, credential verification, and placement matching — either retained on the Enlyst team, referred to a client, or placed into the 10k+ candidate network with a verified skills profile.

Eligibility

Who can apply.

Final-year undergraduates or recent graduates (within 24 months of completion) in Computer Science, IT, Engineering, Data Science, or related fields.

Candidates from any Pakistani university — LUMS, NUST, UET, UMT, COMSATS, and all provincial universities are eligible.

Some exposure to cloud platforms, Linux, or basic programming — full certification training is provided, but baseline technical literacy is required.

Commitment to the full 6-month programme and willingness to work on government and enterprise client projects under professional accountability standards.

No industry experience required — this programme is the entry point.

What you walk away with.

1–2 industry certifications

Google Cloud or Microsoft Azure credentials earned and verifiable on Credly.

A real project portfolio

Work delivered for live government or enterprise clients — not mock projects.

Access to the Enlyst talent network

Your profile enters a 10k+ candidate pipeline connected to 20+ partner organisations.

A defined next step

Retention offer, client referral, or a verified placement — not just a certificate of completion.
